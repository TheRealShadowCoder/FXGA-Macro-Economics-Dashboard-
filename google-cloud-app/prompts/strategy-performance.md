# FXGA Strategy Performance Prompt

## Objective
Evaluate strategy performance only from persisted lifecycle outcomes, event-study results and stored research evidence. Never infer profitability from signal count, score, visual attractiveness, or a small winning streak.

## Analysis requirements
- Separate total signals, filled signals, completed targets, invalidations, expiries and missed entries.
- Where outcome data supports it, calculate or explain hit rates, conditional success rates, expectancy components, average reward/risk, adverse/favourable excursion, drawdown, streaks and regime dependence.
- Distinguish gross directional accuracy from executable strategy performance.
- Separate in-sample, out-of-sample, walk-forward and live-forward evidence when available.
- Identify survivorship, look-ahead, selection, multiple-testing and sparse-sample risks.
- Break results down by setup family, method, symbol, timeframe, session, trend/counter-trend state, event proximity and market regime when the evidence permits.
- Never call a strategy profitable unless actual measured performance supports that statement.

## Output
Use **Performance snapshot**, **What is actually validated**, **Weaknesses / uncertainty**, **Best-performing conditions**, **Poor-performing conditions**, **What to test next**, and a short **Plain-English conclusion**.

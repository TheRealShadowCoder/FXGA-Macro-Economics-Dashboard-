# FXGA Edge Research Prompt

## Objective
Search for measurable conditional advantage in the stored FXGA evidence without manufacturing one. Treat an edge as a hypothesis until it survives sufficient sample size and robustness checks.

## Method
- Compare outcomes by method family, setup archetype, score bucket, symbol, timeframe, session, volatility regime, trend alignment, macro regime and event proximity when those fields exist.
- Look for conditional expectancy rather than raw win rate alone.
- Require sample size, dispersion and uncertainty. Flag fragile findings driven by a few observations.
- Prefer effects that persist across subperiods, instruments and reasonable parameter variations.
- Distinguish descriptive correlation from causal or tradable edge.
- Consider multiple-testing risk and false discovery when many slices are inspected.
- Highlight candidate filters that reduce poor setups only when evidence supports them.
- Recommend OOS, walk-forward, bootstrap, Monte Carlo, regime holdout and sensitivity tests for any promising hypothesis.

## Output
Use **Candidate edges**, **Evidence supporting them**, **Evidence against them**, **Robustness status**, **Unproven hypotheses**, **Next validation experiments**, and **Plain-English summary**. Never label an unvalidated pattern as a guaranteed advantage.

# FXGA Trade Setup Prompt

## Objective
Explain a stored FXGA setup as a complete evidence-based trade hypothesis without creating a new signal or changing its direction.

## Required analysis
- Identify instrument, side, timeframe and lifecycle state.
- Explain higher-timeframe direction, confirmation timeframe, entry timeframe and whether alignment is intact.
- Explain the detected method/archetype, method score and exact-match evidence.
- Describe entry geometry, stop/invalidation, TP1/TP2/TP3 and risk/reward only from stored values.
- Separate original signal evidence from current market evidence.
- Identify what has strengthened or weakened since the signal was generated.
- Explain event risk and nearby macro releases when supplied.
- If the entry was missed, expired, invalidated or already completed, say so clearly and do not treat it as a fresh opportunity.
- Give conditional bullish/bearish continuation and failure scenarios only when supported.

## Output
Use **Setup status**, **Why the setup exists**, **Entry / risk / targets**, **Current confirmation**, **Invalidation**, **Forecast scenarios**, **What would improve the setup**, and **Simple explanation**.

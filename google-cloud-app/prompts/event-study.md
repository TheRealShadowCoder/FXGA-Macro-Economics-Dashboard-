# FXGA Event Study Prompt

Evaluate the maturity and meaning of stored event-study research. Focus on sample coverage, event definitions, pre-event windows, post-event horizons, asset universe, descriptive statistics, validation status and whether results are in-sample or out-of-sample.

Do not equate a repeated historical pattern with a profitable strategy. Flag sparse samples, unstable effects, multiple-testing risk, regime dependence and missing transaction/execution assumptions.

Return **Research coverage**, **Measured patterns**, **Validation status**, **What is not proven**, **Robustness risks**, **Next research tests**, and **Plain-English conclusion**.

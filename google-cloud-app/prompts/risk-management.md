# FXGA Risk Management Prompt

Explain the stored risk geometry and invalidation evidence for research purposes. Use only supplied entry, stop/invalidation, target and risk/reward values. Do not invent position size, leverage, account risk or new order instructions.

Evaluate whether the stored geometry is internally coherent, whether current evidence has weakened the original premise, and whether event risk or structural change materially affects the interpretation. If required values are missing, say what cannot be evaluated.

Return **Recorded risk geometry**, **Structural invalidation**, **Reward/risk evidence**, **Current risks**, **Missing information**, and **Plain-language explanation**.
